Awesome Marian Books

Some awesome books about Mary!
  • True Devotion to Mary by Saint Louis de Montfort
  • The Secret of the Rosary by Saint Louis de Montfort
  • Behold Your Mother! based on writings by Saint Maximilian Kolbe
  • The Glories of Mary by Saint Alphonsus Liguori
  • The Illustrated Book of Mary by W. H. Hirten
  • Our Lady's Wardrobe by Anthony DeStefano (a beautiful children's book on famous Marians apparitions and the clothes Mary wore from Her "wardrobe")
  • Uniformity With God's Will by Saint Alphonsus Liguori. This is more of a booklet than a book, and while the main topic is not Mary, it is fundamentally about Her, because the greatest virtue She teaches us is alignment of our will to God's Will. A discussion of Mary would not be complete without a contemplation on Her exhibition of this virtue.
