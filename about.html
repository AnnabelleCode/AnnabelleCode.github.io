"Do whatever he tells you." (Jn 2:5) 1

Why an entire website dedicated to Mary, some may wonder? For many Christians and non-Christians alike, a great love for Mary can be puzzling when we believe in Jesus Christ as Lord - that we have everything we need from him.

Mary is a quiet figure in Scripture, but think about it: She was and is God's Mother! This means that She has a relationship with Him no one else has or will ever have, and through God's generous love, He has given to us a powerful intercessor, a Mother. In many Catholic schools of thought, Mary is described to be the Moon, while God the Sun. Just as the Sun's light to us can sometimes blind us, God's goodness and power can sometimes seem overwhelming. Yet the Moon possesses a gentler light, while receiving and reflecting this light from the Sun. Just so, Mary perfectly reflects God's love and goodness while being still pure creature - one of us. Many find comfort in Her because of that.

Do Catholics worship Mary? The answer is a resounding "No." Catholics honor Her, respect Her, and love Her as the greatest Saint among the saints - "Queen of Saints," because in Her and through Her obedience and humility, God accomplished in Her what He desired to accomplish in the first human: perfect holiness. Mary's light and greatness never comes from Herself, and Her Will always aligns with the Will of Her Son, Jesus. When we look to Her, She points us to our Lord (just like at the Wedding at Cana in John 2:5) and trains us to be virtuous in love as She was and is.

But, some may wonder, why pray to Mary when we can pray directly to God? Most Christians have had the experience of asking someone to pray for them, or of being asked to pray for someone. Perhaps one has been asked to pray for a friend's exam, that it may go well. Or perhaps one has asked a friend to pray for a sick family member. Intercessory prayer is powerful! And if it's powerful when we, imperfect creatures, pray for each other, how much more powerful is it when Mary, a sinless and perfect human, Mother of God, prays for each of us?

Mary is your Mother and my Mother. Let us love Her together!

Our Lady, Mother of Mankind, pray for us.
